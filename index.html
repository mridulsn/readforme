About readforme

readforme reads text aloud using the voices already on your phone or computer. It goes sentence by sentence, lights up each word, and lets you go back a sentence if you missed something.

Make the text as big as you need with A+. The speed, voice and size are remembered on this device; the text is not.

Which voices you get depends on your device. Phones usually have Indian English and Hindi voices; add more in your phone's text-to-speech settings.
